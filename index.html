Unique culinary experience

At Shima, we provide our guests with a unique culinary experience that goes beyond delicious cuisine. We deliver a theatrical dining event — an art form — that we are confident will exceed our guests’ expectations. We are eager to bring people together for fun and authentic Japanese cuisine with our own modern twist.

About Us

VIP ROOMS

Highest Quality
Ingredients

A modern venue conveniently located in Petitenget, Shima provides the ultimate Japanese cuisine dining with highest quality ingredients prepared by internationally trained chefs. Shima serves premium quality imported Australian beef, Wagyu beef, locally caught fish delivered fresh daily and locally grown vegetables alongside an extensive list of imported and local wines and delicious cocktails.

Authentic Teppanyaki

We are proud to be the only free-standing, authentic Teppanyaki restaurant in Bali and place great emphasis on quality and service. Whether you are enjoying a romantic night out or dining out with friends or family, Shima is the ideal place in Bali to be entertained while enjoying outstanding food and drinks, served by a friendly and experienced staff.
